# Everyday Conversation English

Vocabulário e expressões para conversas informais — small talk, planos, opiniões, gírias.

---

## Small talk (bate-papo leve)

### Cumprimentos além de "Hello"
- "Hey, how's it going?" → "Pretty good, thanks! You?"
- "What's up?" → "Not much, just chilling."
- "How are you doing?" → "Doing well, thanks."
- "Long time no see!" → "I know, right? It's been ages!"
- "How was your weekend?" → "It was great! I..."

### Quando não tem muito a dizer
- "Same old, same old."
- "Can't complain."
- "Keeping busy."
- "Just hanging in there."

---

## Falar sobre planos

### Perguntar
- "What are you up to this weekend?"
- "Any plans for [the holiday / the long weekend]?"
- "Do you want to grab [coffee / lunch / a drink]?"
- "Are you free on Saturday?"

### Responder
- "I'm thinking of [going to...] — want to join?"
- "Nothing concrete yet."
- "I might [go hiking], not sure yet."
- "I'll probably just stay in and relax."
- "I'm actually pretty slammed this week."

---

## Dar opinião

### Concordar
- "I totally agree."
- "Exactly!" / "Precisely!"
- "That's so true."
- "Couldn't agree more."
- "You make a good point."

### Discordar com leveza
- "I'm not sure about that."
- "I see it a bit differently."
- "That's fair, but..."
- "I kind of disagree — I think..."
- "I don't know, I feel like..."

### Dar opinião sobre algo
- "Honestly, I think..."
- "If you ask me..."
- "I feel like..."
- "To be honest, I wasn't that impressed."
- "It was actually better than I expected."

---

## Falar sobre gostos e preferências

- "I'm really into [música / série / esporte]."
- "I've been obsessed with [X] lately."
- "I'm not really a [fan of / into] [X]."
- "I'm more of a [homebody / outdoorsy person]."
- "It's not really my thing."
- "I could take it or leave it."
- "I'm hooked on [série/livro]."

---

## Recomendar coisas

- "You have to try [X]!"
- "I highly recommend [X]."
- "Trust me, it's worth it."
- "You'd love it."
- "It's a bit of a hidden gem."
- "Don't judge it by the [cover / trailer] — it gets good."
- "It's not for everyone, but I loved it."

---

## Contar histórias (storytelling)

### Abrir
- "So, the other day..."
- "You won't believe what happened..."
- "Funny story, actually..."
- "I was just [doing X] when..."

### Desenvolver
- "And then, out of nowhere..."
- "At first I thought..."
- "Turns out..."
- "Long story short..."
- "The crazy part was..."

### Fechar
- "Anyway, it all worked out."
- "And that's how I ended up [doing X]."
- "Lesson learned, I guess."

## Reagir ao que o outro diz

| Situação | Reações |
|---|---|
| Surpresa | "No way!" / "Seriously?" / "You're kidding!" |
| Interesse | "Oh wow." / "That's so cool!" / "Tell me more." |
| Empatia | "Oh no." / "That sucks." / "I'm sorry to hear that." |
| Celebrar com alguém | "That's amazing!" / "I'm so happy for you!" |
| Concordar com entusiasmo | "Right?!" / "I know!" / "Same!" |
| Acompanhar | "Uh-huh." / "Yeah, yeah." / "Mmm." / "Got it." |

## Transições e conectores naturais

- "By the way..." (mudança de assunto)
- "Speaking of which..." (aproveitando o assunto)
- "Actually..." (corrigir ou adicionar info)
- "On that note..." (conectar ao que foi dito)
- "Anyway..." (voltar ao ponto ou encerrar)
- "That said..." (fazer ressalva)
- "I mean..." (clarificar)
