# Business English

Frases e vocabulário para comunicação profissional em inglês — especialmente útil para trabalho remoto com times internacionais.

---

## E-mails e mensagens no trabalho

### Iniciar
- "Hope you're doing well!"
- "Just following up on..."
- "I wanted to reach out about..."
- "Quick question for you..."
- "Heads up — ..."

### Concordar / confirmar
- "Sounds good to me."
- "That works."
- "I'm on board with that."
- "Makes sense."
- "Noted."

### Discordar com tato
- "I see where you're coming from, but..."
- "That's a fair point. However..."
- "I'd push back a little on that..."
- "What if we considered [alternativa] instead?"

### Pedir / delegar
- "Could you take a look at...?"
- "Would you be able to...?"
- "I was wondering if you could..."
- "No rush, but when you get a chance..."

### Encerrar
- "Let me know if you have any questions."
- "Happy to jump on a call if that's easier."
- "Let me know your thoughts."
- "Looking forward to hearing from you."

---

## Reuniões e calls

### Abrir
- "Let's get started."
- "Can everyone hear me okay?"
- "Quick housekeeping before we begin..."

### Durante
- "Sorry to interrupt, but..."
- "Building on what [nome] said..."
- "To clarify..."
- "Can you say that again? I didn't catch that."
- "Can you repeat that? The connection cut out."
- "I'll share my screen."

### Encerrar
- "Let's wrap up."
- "To summarize the action items..."
- "Who's owning this?"
- "I'll send the notes after the call."

---

## Trabalho remoto e assíncrono

- "I'll be OOO (out of office) on Friday."
- "Async message — no need to reply right away."
- "I'm blocked on this, waiting for [pessoa]."
- "I'll pick this up after the meeting."
- "Let's take this offline."
- "I'll loop you in on the thread."
- "Adding [nome] to the CC."

---

## Feedback e code review

- "This looks good overall. One small thing..."
- "Nit: [comentário menor]"
- "Have you considered...?"
- "I'd suggest..."
- "LGTM (Looks Good To Me)"
- "This might cause issues if..."
- "Great catch!"

---

## Escalar e reportar status

- "Just a quick update on [projeto]..."
- "We're on track for [data]."
- "We're slightly behind — here's the plan to catch up."
- "Blocker: [descrição]. I'll need help from [pessoa/time]."
- "This is at risk. Let me flag it early."

---

## Expressões de engajamento (para parecer fluente)

- "Totally." / "Absolutely." / "For sure."
- "That's fair." / "That makes sense."
- "Good point."
- "Right, exactly."
- "I hear you."
- "To be honest..." / "Honestly..."
- "At the end of the day..."

---

## Register: formal vs informal

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| I would like to... | I'd like to... / I wanna... |
| Could you please... | Can you... |
| I am writing to inform you | Just a heads up |
| I appreciate your assistance | Thanks a lot |
| Please find attached | Here's the file |
| Kind regards | Cheers / Talk soon |
