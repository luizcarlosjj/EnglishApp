# Travel English

Vocabulário e frases para viagens internacionais — hotel, voo, transporte, turismo.

---

## Hotel

### Check-in
- "I have a reservation under [nome]."
- "I'd like to check in, please."
- "Is the room ready?"
- "What time is check-out?"

### Problemas
- "There seems to be an issue with my room."
- "The [AC / shower / TV] isn't working."
- "Could you send someone to fix it?"
- "I'd like to request a different room."
- "Is it possible to get an upgrade?"
- "I was promised a [sea view / king bed]."

### Pedir coisas
- "Could I get an extra towel / pillow?"
- "Can you arrange a wake-up call for 7am?"
- "Is breakfast included?"
- "Is there parking available?"

### Reclamar com elegância
- "I understand it's busy, but this is quite inconvenient."
- "I'd appreciate it if you could..."
- "As compensation, would it be possible to...?"
- "I'd like to speak with the manager, please."

---

## Aeroporto e voo

### Check-in / embarque
- "I'd like a window seat, please."
- "How much carry-on is allowed?"
- "Is my connection tight?"
- "Has the gate changed?"

### Atraso / problema
- "My flight has been delayed. What are my options?"
- "I missed my connection because of the delay."
- "Am I entitled to compensation?"
- "Under EU261, passengers are entitled to..."
- "I'd like to request a meal voucher."
- "Can you rebook me on the next available flight?"

### Bagagem
- "My luggage hasn't arrived."
- "I'd like to file a lost baggage claim."
- "My bag was damaged."

---

## Airbnb / hospedagem alternativa

- "Where do I find the key?"
- "Is there parking nearby?"
- "What's the WiFi password?"
- "The [item] seems to be broken."
- "Can you recommend a good place to eat nearby?"
- "What's there to do in the area?"
- "Is [place] walkable from here?"
- "Is there a local market nearby?"

---

## Transporte

- "How do I get to [lugar]?"
- "How long does it take?"
- "Is there a direct route, or do I need to transfer?"
- "Does this bus/train go to...?"
- "One ticket to [destino], please."
- "Return or one-way?"
- "Could you take me to [endereço]?"
- "Could you wait here for a moment?"

---

## Restaurante

- "A table for two, please."
- "We have a reservation under [nome]."
- "Could we see the menu?"
- "What do you recommend?"
- "I'm allergic to [alimento]."
- "Does this contain [alimento]?"
- "Could I have [prato] without [ingrediente]?"
- "Could we get the bill, please?"
- "Is service included?"
- "Could we split the bill?"

---

## Expressões úteis no geral

- "Excuse me, could you help me?"
- "I'm a bit lost — could you point me to...?"
- "Do you speak English?"
- "Could you speak more slowly, please?"
- "Could you write that down for me?"
- "Sorry, I didn't catch that."

---

## Erros comuns

| Errado | Certo |
|---|---|
| I want a room | I'd like a room, please |
| My flight was delayed in 3 hours | My flight was delayed by 3 hours |
| I am here since yesterday | I've been here since yesterday |
| The hotel is very near | The hotel is very close / nearby |
| Make the check-in | Check in (verbo sem "make") |
