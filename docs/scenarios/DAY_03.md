# DAY_03 — Sports Debate with a Friend (Debate Esportivo com Amigo)

**Nível:** Challenging (B2/C1) | **Categoria:** Day to Day | **Emoji:** ⚽

## Visão geral

Jordan Blake, seu amigo australiano que é jornalista esportivo, te manda mensagem logo depois de um jogo da Champions League para debater o que rolou. Ele tem opiniões fortes e quer que você desafie as dele. Mensagens rápidas, tom animado, gírias.

**Quem começa:** Jordan  
**Objetivo:** Ter uma troca de ida e volta com pelo menos 2 opiniões claramente expressas e resposta natural a pushback.

---

## Persona

**Jordan Blake** — Jornalista esportivo, 26 anos, Melbourne.  
Estilo: mensagens rápidas, gírias australianas (reckon, heaps, mate), opiniões fortes, usa "tbh" e "ngl".  
Comportamento: ama ser desafiado; fica entediado com respostas neutras.

---

## Gírias australianas para entender e usar

| Expressão | Significado |
|---|---|
| reckon | achar, acreditar ("I reckon he's overrated") |
| heaps | muito, bastante ("heaps better") |
| mate | amigo, cara |
| ngl (not gonna lie) | para ser honesto |
| tbh (to be honest) | para ser honesto |
| deadset | sério, de verdade |
| no worries | sem problema |

---

## Vocabulário-chave para praticar

| Termo | Como usar |
|---|---|
| reckon | "I reckon the ref made the right call." |
| honestly | "Honestly, I thought they played well." |
| in my opinion | "In my opinion, the manager got it wrong." |
| fair enough | "Fair enough, but..." |
| to be honest | "To be honest, I wasn't impressed." |
| I'd argue | "I'd argue the midfield was the real problem." |
| controversial | "That was a pretty controversial call." |
| you have to admit | "You have to admit, the second goal was quality." |

---

## Foco gramatical

**Dar opiniões com força:**
- "I think [X] was the biggest problem."
- "Honestly, [X] was terrible."
- "In my opinion, they should have..."

**Discordar com naturalidade:**
- "I hear you, but..."
- "That's fair, but I'd argue..."
- "I don't know about that — I thought..."

**Concordar parcialmente:**
- "You're not wrong, but..."
- "Fair point. Although..."
- "I get what you're saying, but..."

**Fazer o debate ir pra frente:**
- "What do you think about [player / decision]?"
- "Did you see that second-half substitution?"
- "Who do you blame the most?"

---

## Frases úteis para este cenário

**Abrir com uma opinião:**
- "I mean, honestly? I thought they were poor for most of the first half too."
- "That penalty was 100% legit though — did you see the replay?"

**Discordar:**
- "Come on, the ref was fine. Our midfield just couldn't hold the ball."
- "I'd argue the bigger issue was the defensive shape, not the officiating."

**Pushback resposta:**
- "Fair enough, but you have to admit the second yellow was harsh."
- "I see your point — but results-wise, it's hard to argue."

**Elevar o debate:**
- "What gets me is the manager's substitutions — too late again."
- "I reckon if they sort out the defensive line they're a different team."

---

## Dicas de sucesso

1. **Tenha uma opinião** — Jordan fica entediado com "é difícil dizer" ou "talvez"
2. **Discorde diretamente** — "I don't agree" é ok; "Fair enough but..." é melhor
3. **Use "to be honest" e "I'd argue"** — soam como fluência real
4. **Ceda às vezes** — "You're not wrong about that" mostra maturidade no debate

---

## Erros comuns de brasileiros neste cenário

| Errado | Certo |
|---|---|
| I am agree | I agree |
| In my opinion I think... | In my opinion... / I think... (não os dois) |
| The team played very bad | The team played really badly |
| Was a bad decision | It was a bad decision |
| I don't liked the referee | I didn't like the referee's decision |

---

## Critério de sucesso

Expressou pelo menos 2 opiniões claras E respondeu ao pushback de Jordan de forma natural (concordando parcialmente ou discordando com argumento).
