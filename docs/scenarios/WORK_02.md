# WORK_02 — Culture Fit Interview (Entrevista de Fit Cultural)

**Nível:** Intermediate (B2) | **Categoria:** Work | **Emoji:** 🤝

## Visão geral

Sofia Andersson, gerente de RH de uma empresa sueca de tech (NordTech), quer um bate-papo informal via WhatsApp antes da rodada final de entrevistas. Ela vai fazer perguntas comportamentais sobre seu estilo de trabalho, como você lida com conflitos e por que quer entrar na empresa.

**Quem começa:** Sofia  
**Objetivo:** Responder pelo menos 2 perguntas comportamentais com exemplos específicos e demonstrar interesse genuíno na empresa.

---

## Persona

**Sofia Andersson** — HR Manager, 34 anos, Estocolmo.  
Estilo: semiformall, faz perguntas de acompanhamento, menciona os valores da empresa.  
Comportamento: fica menos engajada com respostas vagas; aprecia exemplos concretos.

---

## Vocabulário-chave para praticar

| Termo | Como usar |
|---|---|
| work style | "My work style is very collaborative." |
| collaborate | "I love to collaborate across teams." |
| ownership | "I take ownership of my tasks." |
| initiative | "I tend to take initiative when..." |
| team player | "I'm a strong team player but also independent." |
| flat hierarchy | "I thrive in a flat hierarchy." |
| feedback | "I'm very open to feedback." |
| deadline | "I manage deadlines by breaking tasks down." |

---

## Método STAR — respostas comportamentais

Estrutura para perguntas "Tell me about a time when…":

| Etapa | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | "So, when I was at [empresa], we had a tight deadline for a product launch..." |
| **Task** | "My task was to coordinate between the dev and design teams." |
| **Action** | "What I did was set up a daily sync and created a shared tracking doc." |
| **Result** | "As a result, we shipped on time and the client was really happy." |

---

## Foco gramatical

**Simple past para contar histórias:**
- "I led, I built, I decided, I noticed, I managed"

**Expressões de consequência:**
- "As a result…" / "Because of that…" / "In the end…"

**Mostrar reflexão:**
- "Looking back, I think…"
- "One thing I learned from that was…"

---

## Frases úteis para este cenário

**Sobre estilo de trabalho:**
- "I work best when I have autonomy, but I also enjoy close collaboration."
- "I'm very deadline-oriented and I communicate proactively when things are at risk."

**Sobre feedback:**
- "I actually really value direct feedback — I find it helps me grow faster."

**Sobre a empresa:**
- "I was impressed by NordTech's approach to remote work — could you tell me more about how teams collaborate day-to-day?"
- "What does success look like for this role in the first 3 months?"

**Transições naturais:**
- "That's a great question — let me think for a second."
- "To give you some context…"

---

## Dicas de sucesso

1. **Sempre use exemplos** — "I value teamwork" diz pouco; "I led a cross-team project at X" diz muito
2. **Pesquise os valores da empresa** — Sofia menciona NordTech values; echo-los
3. **Faça perguntas inteligentes** — mostra que você está engajado e é senior-minded
4. **Evite críticas negativas de ex-empregadores** — diga "I was looking for more growth" não "meu chefe era péssimo"

---

## Erros comuns de brasileiros neste cenário

| Errado | Certo |
|---|---|
| I am agree with that | I agree with that |
| I like work in team | I like working in a team |
| I did this at my last work | I did this at my last job |
| I am a person who likes... | I tend to... / I'm someone who... |
| I have proactivity | I'm proactive / I take initiative |

---

## Critério de sucesso

Respondeu pelo menos 2 perguntas comportamentais com exemplos específicos E demonstrou interesse genuíno na empresa com pelo menos 1 pergunta.
